## Visual settings

- Dark, near-black navy #020d20 with restrained #04152a center. White explanatory type; selective orange title phrase; cyan/violet and service-specific colors carry meaning. No excessive yellow.
- At 2048x1152 use proportional title about 60-64px, premise about 34px, support about 30-32px. Preserve normal-view readability; trim repetitions before shrinking further. The approved smaller text remains clear.
- Crisp edges and lettering, fine solid outlines, restrained depth on meaningful pictograms. No neon bloom, text glow, thick glass frames or oversized highlights.
- Use a teaching claim, a concrete example and accurately directed relationships. Decide composition from the content: architecture, annotated evidence, comparison, timeline, request/response or recovery. Tables and numbered steps only when the concept needs them. No compulsory bottom strip, card grid or three-stage shell.
- Match educational density and explanatory phrasing of original AIP examples. Useful supporting text is required; neither decoration nor sparse labels replace teaching.
