New visuals explain the assigned topic in original wording and retain the `visual-review` status; reused visuals also retain that status until each visual's teaching depth and exact technical wording are inspected against the source and current AWS documentation. Page metadata coverage alone is not a claim that every source subtopic or current DVA-C03 objective has passed semantic QA.

The 26 original diagrams were redesigned against the supplied SAP visual references. They now use topic-specific comparison, branch, diagnostic, security-boundary, release and request-path compositions; glossy dark backgrounds, luminous service illustrations, prominent white/orange title hierarchy, detailed explanation and decision tables, and a Remember takeaway. This is an image and metadata review, not a certification of every current service exception or source-PDF subtopic.
